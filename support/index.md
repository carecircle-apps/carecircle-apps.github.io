# Juntos support

Juntos is operated by Ricardo Ochoa. For app support, account assistance or privacy requests, email [carecircle89@icloud.com](mailto:carecircle89@icloud.com).

Please include your app version, iPhone model, and a description of the issue. Do not email passwords, payment-card details, or patient medical records. Remove personal information from screenshots before sending them.

## Account and circle access

Sign in using the email associated with your Juntos account. Use the password-reset option on the sign-in screen if needed. Circle invitations come from a caregiver in that circle. Both caregiver and family requests require approval from the original circle creator before access; a family invitation provides the family view after approval.

## Chat reports and blocking

Use the controls beside a Chat message to report it with a reason or block the sender. Manage blocked members from Chat. A block hides messages between those two accounts; it does not remove circle membership or hide other shared care features. Reporting copies the selected message and its identifiers to a private Supabase review queue; it does not send the message to AI or automatically forward it by email.

Ricardo Ochoa reviews the queue daily and within 24 hours. Confirmed violations may lead to message removal or suspension of chat posting. A narrow automated phrase filter also rejects certain abusive messages, but cannot detect every problem. This is not an emergency service. Closed reports are manually removed from the active database within 30 days after closure; this operator task is not automated, and provider backups may retain earlier copies.

For support about a report, email the support address above without copying patient records or the full conversation.

## AI allowance

Each circle shares 25 free AI answers across its approved members. Joining or rejoining does not add questions. At the introduction of this shared allowance, existing circles receive one new 25-answer launch allocation; historical per-account usage remains separate. This does not start an Apple subscription. Paid question allowances belong to the purchasing account. Manual Roadmap updates have a separate 10-successful-check trial per account; they do not use AI question credits.

## Subscriptions

AI subscriptions are optional. Open AI options in the app to see plans, restore purchases or manage your subscription. Apple handles purchases. You can also manage or cancel subscriptions in your iPhone Settings under your Apple Account > Subscriptions. Deleting Juntos or its account does not cancel an Apple subscription.

## Account deletion and privacy

Caregivers can delete their account in More > Security > Delete Account. Family members can use the Account screen. Shared care history remains available to remaining circle members; names or information written into shared text may remain. Contact us for privacy or data-access requests involving that content. Do not include the care records in your initial email.

Read the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy/) and [Terms of Use](/legal/terms/).

## Medical emergencies

Juntos helps families coordinate care. It does not provide emergency response or replace your healthcare team. For an emergency, contact local emergency services.
